I am an Assistant Professor in Interactive Computing at Georgia Tech, where I direct the D3 Lab. My research focuses on human-centered AI and health/well-being in underserved communities. I examine how we can design for more just care infrastructures that better recognize the labor, autonomy, and rights of care workers and care recipients.

My research has been published and recognized at leading HCI venues such as ACM CHI, ACM CSCW, and ACM FAccT. This work has been supported by Google, the Atlanta Interdisciplinary AI Network, and others, and has received coverage in media outlets across the US.

I was previously a postdoctoral fellow at the Harvard Center for Research on Computation and Society. I have a PhD in Computer Science from the University of Washington and an undergraduate degree in Computer Science from Georgia Tech.

I also think a lot about care and work outside of research. I serve as Vice President for Communications on the SIGCHI Executive Committee, where I support an informed and connected HCI research community.
